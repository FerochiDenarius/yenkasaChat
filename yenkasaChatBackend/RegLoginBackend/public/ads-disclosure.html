Yenkasa Rewards & Ads Transparency

Yenkasa uses a virtual reward system called YKC (Yenkasa Coins). These rewards are designed to encourage positive engagement within the community. YKC has no real-world monetary value and cannot be exchanged for cash.

💬 Comments & Replies

  • Posting a comment: +5 YKC
  • Receiving a reply to your comment: +2 YKC
  • Liking a comment: +1 YKC
  • Receiving a like on your comment: +1 YKC

👥 Following Users

  • Following another user: +10 YKC
  • Being followed by another user: +5 YKC

📝 Posts & Engagement

  • Creating a post (approved): +10 YKC
  • Receiving a like on your post: +2 YKC
  • Receiving a comment on your post: +3 YKC

👁️ Content Views

Rewards depend on content type and watch duration:

  • Image views
    • 3 seconds: +1 YKC
    • 5 seconds or more: +2 YKC
  • Video views
    • 10 seconds: +2 YKC
    • 30 seconds: +5 YKC
    • 60 seconds: +10 YKC
    • 120 seconds or more: +20 YKC
  • Audio views
    • 20 seconds: +2 YKC
    • 45 seconds: +5 YKC
    • 90 seconds or more: +10 YKC
  • Post owners receive +1 YKC per view from other users.

🏘️ Communities

  • Joining a community: +5 YKC
  • Creating a community: +100 YKC (pending approval)
  • Community approval by admin: +10 YKC

🛡️ Moderation & Administration

  • Approving a post (admin/moderator): +10 YKC
  • Rejecting a post (admin/moderator): +10 YKC

📢 Advertisements

Yenkasa may display sponsored content or ads. Some ads may offer optional in-app rewards for engagement such as viewing.

Watching ads is never mandatory and does not guarantee real-world earnings.

⚠️ Rewards may be adjusted over time to ensure fairness, prevent abuse, and improve the platform. Abuse of the reward system may result in suspension or loss of rewards.
