Ads & Rewards – How Ads Work on Yenkasa

Yenkasa displays advertisements (including rewarded ads) to help support the platform and local creators. Ads may be provided by Google AdMob and other partners.

What Ad Partners May Collect

  • Advertising identifiers (Android Advertising ID)
  • Approximate location (via IP)
  • App usage related to ad impressions and clicks

Personalized Ads

You may see personalized ads based on interests and activity. You can disable ad personalization in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Rewarded Ads

Optional rewarded ads let you earn YKC for watching sponsored videos. Rewards are subject to verification and fraud prevention.
