5 Mistakes Fashion Vendors Make That Kill Their Sales (And How to Fix Them)

👁 0 views | ❤️ 0 likes

If your fashion business is not growing, you might be making one of these mistakes. The good news is that each one can be fixed.

Yenkasa Store Team

1. Relying Only on WhatsApp

WhatsApp is useful, but it limits your growth when it is your only selling channel. Customers can miss your status, old products disappear, and you keep answering the same questions every day.

Fix this by listing your products on a platform where more people can see them anytime.

2. Poor Product Visibility

If people cannot see your products clearly, they will not buy. Poor photos, missing prices, unclear sizes, and scattered posts all reduce sales.

Fix this by using platforms like Yenkasa Store to showcase your items properly with product names, prices, images, and descriptions.

3. No Trust System

Customers are scared of scams, fake sellers, wrong sizes, and poor quality. If your business does not show trust signals, many interested buyers will not pay.

Fix this by using secure platforms with organized product listings and payment systems that make buyers feel safer.

4. Slow Response to Customers

You cannot reply to everyone instantly, especially when you are working, sourcing stock, packing orders, or handling delivery. Slow replies can make buyers move to another seller.

Fix this by making your products available online 24/7. A buyer should be able to see product details even before you reply.

5. No Proper Online Presence

If you are not online properly, you are invisible to many buyers. Posting once in a while is not the same as building a real online presence.

Fix this by creating a store where customers can always find you and browse your products.
