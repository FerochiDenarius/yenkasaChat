Sell More Shoes Without Dropping Your Price

👁 0 views | ❤️ 0 likes

Your shoes may be nice. Your price may be fair. But if only a few people see them, sales will still be slow.

Yenkasa Store Team

The problem is not always your price

Many sellers reduce prices too quickly. But sometimes the real issue is simple: not enough serious buyers are seeing your shoes.

Truth: Better visibility can help you protect your profit.

What buyers want to see fast

  • Clear shoe pictures
  • Available sizes
  • Price
  • Location or delivery option
  • Simple way to order

WhatsApp status is not enough

Some customers miss your status. Some view and forget. Some want to check later, but cannot find the item again.

Your shoes need a proper online listing where people can browse anytime.

Put your shoes on Yenkasa Store

On Yenkasa Store, you can upload your shoes and make them easier for buyers to find.

Smart move: Keep using WhatsApp, but add Yenkasa Store as another sales channel.

Sell smarter, not cheaper

Before reducing your price, improve your photos, show sizes clearly, and list your products online.
