Top Trending Fashion Styles in Ghana Right Now (2026 Edition)

Fashion in Ghana keeps evolving fast, and if you want to stay ahead, you need to know what is trending right now.

Yenkasa Store Team

Whether you are shopping for yourself or looking for affordable styles to resell, here are the hottest fashion trends dominating Ghana in 2026.

1. Oversized T-Shirts and Streetwear

Oversized tees, graphic prints, and relaxed fits are everywhere. This style is perfect for casual outings, campus looks, weekend events, and everyday comfort.

You can find affordable oversized tees on Yenkasa Store right now.

2. Poly Silk Tops for Ladies

Poly silk tops are trending because they are elegant, comfortable, and perfect for both office wear and outings. They give that clean premium look without forcing shoppers to spend too much.

For vendors, poly silk tops are a strong product category because they appeal to students, office workers, churchgoers, and women who want flexible wardrobe pieces.

3. Thrift Fashion and Bend Down Select

Thrift fashion is booming in Ghana. Many people now prefer second-hand clothes because they are cheaper, unique, and often high quality when sourced well.

For sellers, thrift fashion works best when items are clean, photographed clearly, and described honestly. Buyers want to know size, condition, color, and any small defects before paying.

4. Lacoste and Round Neck Shirts

Simple, clean, and classy, Lacoste and round neck shirts remain a top choice for men in Ghana. They work for casual Friday, weekend events, date nights, and everyday smart casual dressing.

If you sell men’s wear, keep colors like black, white, navy, grey, wine, and forest green in mind. These colors move well because they are easy to style.
