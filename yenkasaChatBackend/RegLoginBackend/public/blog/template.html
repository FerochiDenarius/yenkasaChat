What every vendor article should do

A strong Yenkasa Store article should solve a real seller problem. It should help someone in Ghana understand how to sell better on Instagram, WhatsApp, marketplaces, MoMo, delivery, trust, customer growth, product photos, or boutique setup.

How the article should guide readers

Each section should use simple language, practical examples, and real Ghana selling context. Readers should leave with clear next steps they can apply the same day, even if they are starting from home with a phone and small stock.

How internal links should work

Articles should connect to related guides such as selling clothes online in Ghana, starting a boutique from home, and building customer trust online.

How conversion should be handled

Readers should be invited to take action without pressure. The CTA should appear naturally after useful advice and again near the end so sellers who are ready can move from learning to listing products.
