Community Guidelines – Yenkasa

Last updated: December 2025

Welcome

Yenkasa is a place for local and interest-based communities. These guidelines help keep the platform respectful and useful.

Be Respectful

No harassment, threats, hate speech, or doxxing.

Content Rules

  • No sexual content involving minors.
  • No graphic violence or illegal content.
  • No copyright infringement or spam.
  • No disallowed commercial solicitation without disclosure.

Rewards & Integrity

YKC rewards are for creating useful content and engaging authentically. Do not:

  • Create fake accounts to farm rewards
  • Coordinate fraudulent interactions to game the reward system
  • Use automated posting or bots

Violations may lead to content removal, reward reversal, or account suspension.

Moderation

Moderation is a mix of automated detection and human review. If your content is removed you can contact support for review.

Reporting

Use the in-app report button to flag violations. We typically review within 24–72 hours.
