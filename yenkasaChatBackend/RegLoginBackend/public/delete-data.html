Request Data Deletion – Yenkasa

Yenkasa allows users to request deletion of specific types of personal data without deleting their account.

What data you can request to delete

  • Posts you created
  • Comments and replies
  • Profile media (profile picture)
  • Location information

What data may be retained

  • Financial or reward transaction records (if required by law)
  • Aggregated analytics (non-identifiable)
  • Security and abuse-prevention logs (limited time)

How to request data deletion

Submit the form below using the email associated with your Yenkasa account. Your request will be reviewed and processed within 30 days.







Requests are processed within 30 days. Some data may be retained if required by law.
