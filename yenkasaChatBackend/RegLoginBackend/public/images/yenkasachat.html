Get The App

Download YenkasaChat (Android APK)

Installation Notes:

After downloading the .apk file:

1. Open your phone's file manager and find the downloaded file.

2. Tap on it to install. You might need to enable "Installation from Unknown Sources" or "Allow apps from this source" in your Android settings if prompted.

(Usually found in Settings > Security or Settings > Apps > Special app access).
