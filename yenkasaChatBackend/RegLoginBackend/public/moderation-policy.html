Moderation Policy – Yenkasa

Last updated: December 2025

Purpose

To keep communities safe and ensure fair reward distribution.

Moderation Process

  • Automated filters scan for spam, abuse, and policy violations.
  • Flagged content is reviewed by human moderators.
  • Repeat or severe violations result in content removal, warnings, and bans.

Reward Abuse Detection

We monitor patterns that indicate manipulation (e.g., sockpuppet accounts, coordinated activity). Suspected abuse may result in reversing rewards and sanctions.

Appeals

Contact support@yenkasa.xyz to request a review. Provide relevant context & IDs.
