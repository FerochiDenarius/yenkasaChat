Safety Policy – Yenkasa

Last updated: December 2025

Safety Tools

  • Report content or users directly in-app
  • Block unwanted accounts
  • Community moderation by admins

Emergency Response

We will cooperate with law enforcement when required by law. If immediate danger is present, contact local authorities.

Protecting Minors & Vulnerable Users

We prohibit sexual content involving minors. We remove predatory or exploitative content immediately.

Moderation Timeline

Most reports reviewed within 24–72 hours. Complex cases may take longer.

Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) Policy

Yenkasa has a zero-tolerance policy against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

  • We prohibit any sexualized content involving minors.
  • We prohibit grooming, exploitation, trafficking, or abuse of children.
  • Any account involved in such activity will be permanently suspended.
  • Content involving child exploitation will be removed immediately.
  • We may preserve evidence and report serious violations to the relevant authorities.

Reporting Child Safety Concerns

Users can report messages, profiles, uploaded content, or harmful behaviour directly within the Yenkasa app.

Reports relating to minors or child safety are prioritized and reviewed as quickly as possible.
