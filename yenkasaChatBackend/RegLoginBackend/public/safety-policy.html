Safety Policy – Yenkasa

Last updated: December 2025

Safety Tools

  • Report content or users directly in-app
  • Block unwanted accounts
  • Community moderation by admins

Emergency Response

We will cooperate with law enforcement when required by law. If immediate danger is present, contact local authorities.

Protecting Minors & Vulnerable Users

We prohibit sexual content involving minors. We remove predatory or exploitative content immediately.

Moderation Timeline

Most reports reviewed within 24–72 hours. Complex cases may take longer.
