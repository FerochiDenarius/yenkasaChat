Yenkasa Store Policies

Privacy Policy, Buyer Terms and Seller Agreement

This page explains how Yenkasa Store handles account information, orders, payments, delivery, refunds, seller commissions and marketplace usage.

Last updated: April 13, 2026

1. Who We Are

Yenkasa Store is a marketplace operated by Yenkasa Soft-O-Tech. The platform allows sellers to list products such as fashion items, bales, fabrics, bags, shoes, wigs, human hair, accessories and other approved products. Buyers can browse, place orders, make payments and arrange delivery through the website.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information needed to operate the marketplace, including:

  • Name, email address, phone number and account role.
  • Delivery address, region, area, landmark and order notes.
  • Seller payout details such as mobile money or bank information.
  • Product details, images, videos, prices, categories and stock status.
  • Payment references, order records, delivery status and support records.
  • Technical information such as browser type, device type, IP address and security logs.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and secure user accounts.
  • Process orders, payments, delivery and customer support.
  • Process product listings, fixed-price checkout, delivery, refunds and platform support.
  • Verify transactions before marking orders as paid.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, spam, fake listings and unauthorized access.
  • Improve the website, product catalogue, search experience and seller tools.

4. Payments and Seller Commission

Yenkasa Store charges a 10% commission on all sales made through the website, unless a promotion or special agreement clearly states otherwise. This commission is used for platform maintenance, hosting, payment processing support, security, customer support and continued marketplace improvements.

Sellers are responsible for listing accurate prices and product details. Buyers should review product details before completing payment. Product prices are fixed unless a seller or Yenkasa Store clearly publishes a discount or promotion on the website.

5. Delivery Policy

Delivery fees are paid by buyers unless the website, seller or promotion clearly states otherwise before checkout. Delivery availability, price and timing may depend on the buyer location, seller location, product type and delivery provider.

Buyers must provide accurate names, phone numbers and delivery addresses. Yenkasa Store may contact buyers or sellers to confirm delivery information before dispatch.

6. Refunds, Rejected Orders and Reviews

Orders rejected by buyers will be reviewed before a refund decision is made. Where a refund is approved, Yenkasa Store aims to process the refund within 48 hours after review. Refund timing may also depend on payment providers, banks, mobile money operators or delivery partners.

Refunds may be refused or reduced where a product was damaged after delivery, buyer information was incorrect, the buyer failed to receive the order without valid reason, or the claim is found to be fraudulent.

7. Seller Responsibilities

Sellers using Yenkasa Store agree to:

  • Post only products they are allowed to sell.
  • Use clear product pictures, accurate descriptions and correct prices.
  • Keep stock status updated as available, preorder or sold.
  • Respond to buyer and platform communication in good time.
  • Provide accurate payout details and delivery information.
  • Avoid counterfeit, illegal, misleading, unsafe or prohibited products.

8. Buyer Responsibilities

Buyers using Yenkasa Store agree to:

  • Use accurate contact and delivery information.
  • Review product details before placing orders.
  • Pay product and delivery fees where applicable.
  • Be available to receive orders or communicate delivery changes early.
  • Report order problems honestly and with supporting information where requested.

9. Seller Contact and Fixed Prices

Public product cards do not display seller WhatsApp contacts for bargaining. Buyers should use the website checkout flow and official support channels. Prices shown on product cards are treated as fixed prices unless a published website discount or promotion states otherwise.

10. Third-Party Services

Yenkasa Store may use third-party services for payments, email delivery, image hosting, analytics, delivery or fraud prevention. These services may process limited information needed to provide their services. Examples may include payment providers, delivery providers, email providers and media storage providers.

11. Account Security

Users are responsible for keeping their login details secure. If you believe your account has been accessed without permission, contact support immediately. Yenkasa Store may suspend, block or restrict accounts involved in abuse, fraud, illegal activity or platform misuse.

12. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep account, order, payment, delivery and seller records for as long as needed to provide the service, resolve disputes, meet business requirements and protect the platform. Users may contact us to request account updates or deletion, subject to records we must keep for security, transaction, dispute or legal reasons.

13. Policy Updates

We may update these policies as the marketplace grows, including when new delivery providers, payment options, seller tools, product categories or legal requirements are introduced. Continued use of the website after updates means you accept the updated terms.

14. Contact

For support, policy questions, refunds, delivery issues or account requests, contact Yenkasa Store support.
