# The Yenkasa Creator Economy

**Audience:** Public users  
**Category:** creator-tools  
**Related topics:** YKC, communities, Live Arena, ranks

## Overview

Yenkasa supports a creator economy built around engagement, visibility, trust, and participation. The platform is designed to help creators grow by building real audience relationships instead of relying only on one-way publishing.

## What Helps Creators Grow

Creators on Yenkasa can benefit from:

- post engagement
- comments and replies
- followers and repeat supporters
- community participation
- livestream activity
- Live Arena visibility
- rank and trust progression
- YKC reward participation

## Creator Roles

Yenkasa also supports creator-oriented public roles such as:

- verified creator
- business account
- premium seller
- campus influencer
- brand ambassador

These roles support more specialized identity and growth paths beyond the main rank ladder.

## Why Creator Growth Is Different on Yenkasa

Yenkasa treats creator growth as part of a wider platform economy. A creator does not grow only from posting. Growth can come from:

- how people respond
- how communities form around content
- livestream energy
- repeated engagement
- trust and consistency

## FAQ

### Is Yenkasa only for influencers?

No. The creator economy is important, but the platform also supports ordinary users, community members, and active supporters.

### Can community activity help creators?

Yes. Communities can concentrate attention and strengthen audience relationships.

### Can livestreams help creator visibility?

Yes. Live activity and Arena participation can increase recognition and engagement.
