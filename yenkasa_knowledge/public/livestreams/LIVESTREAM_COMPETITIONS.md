# Livestream Competitions on Yenkasa

**Audience:** Public users  
**Category:** livestreams  
**Related topics:** Live Arena, YKC, titles, leaderboards

## Overview

Yenkasa livestream competitions add structured rivalry and reward loops to live sessions. One of the main competition formats is the live duel system.

## Live Duels

Live duels are timed head-to-head competition moments between livestream participants. They are designed to create urgency, energy, and audience participation.

## What Duels Emphasize

Duels can focus on activity such as:

- comments
- views
- engagement intensity

The exact competition signal depends on the duel setup.

## Micro Rewards

Yenkasa also supports micro reward behavior during live competition windows. This helps make participation feel more immediate and dynamic.

Examples of live competition rewards include:

- duel reward moments
- comment-based micro rewards
- view-based micro rewards

## Titles and Recognition

Strong duel performers can earn recognition titles such as:

- Arena Rising
- Live Beast

These titles reinforce the competitive side of livestream participation.

## FAQ

### Do livestream competitions change the normal stream?

They add an event layer on top of the normal livestream experience.

### Are livestream competitions only for creators?

Hosts are central, but audience activity also matters.

### Why does Yenkasa use competitions?

They increase engagement, energy, repeat participation, and creator-audience interaction.
