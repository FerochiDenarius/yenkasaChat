# What Is Yenkasa Live Arena?

**Audience:** Public users  
**Category:** livestreams  
**Related topics:** livestream competitions, leaderboards, YKC

## Overview

Yenkasa Live Arena is the platform’s competitive live engagement layer. It turns livestream participation into a more active, visible, and reward-aware experience.

Instead of treating livestreaming as a passive watch room, Live Arena highlights top activity and competition in real time.

## What Live Arena Tracks

Live Arena can surface top activity around:

- comments
- post likes
- views given
- follows and connections
- YKC earned

This creates a more game-like and event-driven livestream environment.

## What Users See

Depending on the live session and event window, users can see:

- top commenters
- top connectors
- top viewers
- top YKC performers
- activity events
- duel status
- micro rewards

## Why Live Arena Exists

Live Arena gives livestreams more energy. It rewards participation, encourages repeat engagement, and gives both hosts and viewers a stronger reason to stay involved.

## FAQ

### Is Live Arena the same as a normal livestream?

No. A normal livestream is the base live experience. Live Arena adds competition, rankings, and activity-based recognition.

### Do viewers matter in Live Arena?

Yes. Viewers, commenters, connectors, and supporters can all influence Arena activity.

### Is Live Arena only for hosts?

No. It is built around both broadcaster and audience participation.
