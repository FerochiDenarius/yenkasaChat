# Moderation and User Safety on Yenkasa

**Audience:** Public users  
**Category:** moderation  
**Related topics:** communities, livestreams, creator tools

## Overview

Yenkasa uses moderation and safety controls to protect users, creators, communities, and livestream spaces.

The goal is to keep the platform useful, respectful, and difficult to abuse.

## What Moderation Covers

Moderation can apply to:

- posts
- comments
- communities
- livestream activity
- role misuse
- reward abuse

## Why Moderation Matters

Without moderation, rewards, visibility, ranks, and community tools can be manipulated. Yenkasa treats safety as part of platform quality, not as an optional extra.

## What Users Should Expect

Users can expect systems that help:

- reduce spam and abuse
- review sensitive content
- protect creators and communities
- keep engagement healthier
- block harmful shortcuts

## Public Safety Principle

YenkasaAI should explain safety and moderation clearly, but it should not teach people how to bypass platform controls.

That means public answers should focus on:

- policy
- user guidance
- healthy behavior
- high-level platform rules

and avoid sharing exploit paths or evasion tactics.

## FAQ

### Does moderation only happen after reports?

No. Moderation can involve both proactive systems and review workflows.

### Why are some features role-gated?

Because features such as community creation, review authority, or livestream control can affect trust and safety across the platform.

### Does moderation protect livestreams too?

Yes. Livestream spaces are part of the safety surface.
