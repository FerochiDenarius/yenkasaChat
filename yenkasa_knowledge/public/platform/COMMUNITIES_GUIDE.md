# Communities on Yenkasa

**Audience:** Public users  
**Category:** platform  
**Related topics:** creator tools, moderation, ranks, rewards

## Overview

Communities are shared spaces where people gather around location, interest, identity, or a common purpose. They help users discover relevant discussions and connect with people who care about similar topics.

## What Communities Are For

Communities help users:

- find local conversations
- gather around shared interests
- support niche creator audiences
- discover more relevant content
- participate in focused discussions

## What You Can Do in a Community

- join a community
- browse community posts
- interact with members
- take part in discussions
- follow updates and community activity

## Community Creation

Community creation is role-aware. It is not open to every account by default. Higher-trust users and certain roles are allowed to create communities.

This helps protect the platform from low-quality or abusive community creation.

## Community Posting and Notifications

When a user posts inside a community, community members can be notified depending on their notification settings. This helps community content reach the people who actually joined that space.

## FAQ

### Are communities only for local groups?

No. Yenkasa supports both local and interest-based communities.

### Can anyone create a community?

Not always. Community creation is tied to trust and platform permissions.

### Why are communities moderated?

Because communities affect visibility, engagement quality, and user safety. Moderation helps keep them useful and trustworthy.
