# Yenkasa Engagement Philosophy

**Audience:** Public users  
**Category:** platform  
**Related topics:** YKC, ranks, moderation, creator economy

## Overview

Yenkasa is built around active participation. The platform aims to reward people who contribute useful energy to the ecosystem instead of treating all activity as equal.

That means actions such as thoughtful comments, consistent logins, helpful interactions, community participation, and creator support matter.

## Core Ideas

### 1. Consistency matters

Yenkasa recognizes repeated, steady activity over time. A user who keeps showing up, engaging, and contributing is more valuable than a user who appears once and disappears.

### 2. Meaningful interaction matters

The platform values:

- real comments
- follows and connections
- post interaction
- community participation
- watch activity

### 3. Healthy growth matters

Yenkasa is not meant to reward abuse, spam, or manipulation. Safety systems and moderation controls exist to keep growth trustworthy.

### 4. Community matters

Communities, comments, livestream participation, and creator support are part of the platform identity. Engagement is not only about publishing posts.

## What This Means for Users

If you want to grow on Yenkasa, focus on:

- staying active
- interacting respectfully
- joining relevant communities
- participating in livestream and Live Arena moments
- creating or supporting useful content

## FAQ

### Does Yenkasa reward only posting?

No. Posting matters, but so do comments, follows, views, consistency, and other interactions.

### Why does the app track multiple metrics?

Because Yenkasa wants a fuller picture of contribution, not a single vanity number.

### Does bad behavior help growth?

No. Moderation and safety systems are intended to block harmful shortcuts and protect the platform.
