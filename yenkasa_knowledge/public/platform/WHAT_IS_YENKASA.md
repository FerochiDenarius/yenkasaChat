# What Is Yenkasa?

**Audience:** Public users  
**Category:** platform  
**Related topics:** YKC, ranks, communities, livestreams, creator tools

## Overview

Yenkasa is a social platform built around community, conversation, discovery, and creator growth. It combines short-form content, comments, livestream activity, communities, rewards, and rank progression into one app experience.

Yenkasa is designed to reward useful participation, not just passive scrolling. The platform tracks activity such as posting, commenting, connecting with others, viewing content, and staying active over time.

## What People Do on Yenkasa

- watch and interact with posts
- join local or interest-based communities
- participate in livestreams and Live Arena activity
- earn Yenkasa Coin (YKC) for meaningful actions
- grow through verification and rank progression
- build creator visibility through engagement and consistency

## What Makes Yenkasa Different

Yenkasa is not only a feed app. It also includes:

- a rewards system
- a rank system
- community participation
- livestream competition features
- moderation and safety controls
- creator growth tools

The product philosophy is that healthy engagement should lead to recognition, better visibility, and stronger communities.

## Common Questions

### Is Yenkasa only for creators?

No. Regular users, commenters, viewers, community members, and creators all have a place in the app.

### Is Yenkasa only about livestreaming?

No. Livestreams are one part of the platform. Posts, comments, communities, rewards, and ranks are also central to the experience.

### Does Yenkasa reward engagement?

Yes. Yenkasa uses YKC, rankings, milestones, and visibility systems to recognize useful activity.

## Quick Summary

Yenkasa is a social platform where users can connect, contribute, grow, earn YKC, join communities, and build reputation through consistent activity.
