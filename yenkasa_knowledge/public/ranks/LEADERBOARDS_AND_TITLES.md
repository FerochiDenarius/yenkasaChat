# Leaderboards and Titles on Yenkasa

**Audience:** Public users  
**Category:** ranks  
**Related topics:** YKC, Live Arena, ranks

## Overview

Yenkasa uses leaderboards and live titles to highlight active contributors, strong performers, and competitive participation.

## YKC Leaderboards

One leaderboard tracks monthly YKC performance. It can rank users by YKC earned and related contribution signals.

This helps users see who is performing strongly inside the reward economy.

## Live Arena Recognition

Live Arena also has live-style rankings and event indicators. Depending on activity, users may be recognized for:

- comments
- likes
- views
- follows
- YKC performance

## Live Titles

Some live titles can appear through duel performance. Examples include:

- Arena Rising
- Live Beast

These titles reflect strong livestream competition results.

## Why Leaderboards Exist

Leaderboards create visibility around consistent contribution and competitive moments. They can motivate participation, but they also help users understand what kinds of activity matter on the platform.

## FAQ

### Are leaderboards only about posting?

No. Different leaderboards can reflect different forms of activity.

### Are live titles permanent ranks?

Not necessarily. Live titles are tied to livestream competition behavior, not the full rank ladder.

### Can YKC and leaderboard performance help visibility?

Yes. Strong performance can increase recognition inside the platform ecosystem.
