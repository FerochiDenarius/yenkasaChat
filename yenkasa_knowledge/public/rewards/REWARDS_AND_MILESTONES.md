# Rewards and Milestones on Yenkasa

**Audience:** Public users  
**Category:** rewards  
**Related topics:** YKC, ranks, verification, leaderboards

## Overview

Yenkasa rewards are designed around progress, not one-time spikes. The system looks at patterns such as activity, engagement, logins, views, comments, and community behavior over time.

## How Rewards Work

Rewards are triggered from trusted backend events. This means Yenkasa does not rely on users self-reporting activity. The platform checks real activity signals before crediting progress or YKC.

## Types of Reward Progress

### Daily progress

Some actions contribute to daily progress, such as:

- daily logins
- watch activity
- interaction streaks

### Contribution progress

Other actions reflect contribution, such as:

- comments made
- follows made
- likes given
- content performance

### Recognition progress

Some progress is about visibility and trust, such as:

- followers gained
- comments received
- consistent participation over time

## Milestones

Milestones help users understand long-term growth. Examples include:

- becoming verified
- moving into rising star or legend levels
- earning more YKC over time
- improving leaderboard position
- building creator or community influence

## Why Milestones Matter

Milestones give users a path. Instead of asking, "What do I do next?" the system can show what is needed for the next level.

## FAQ

### Are rewards instant for everything?

Not always. Some rewards are immediate, while others depend on validated activity and progression.

### Can rewards be capped?

Yes. Daily or action-specific caps can apply.

### Are milestones the same as ranks?

Not exactly. Milestones are progress markers. Ranks are broader trust and contribution levels.
