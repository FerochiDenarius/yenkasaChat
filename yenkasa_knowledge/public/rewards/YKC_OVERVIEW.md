# What Is Yenkasa Coin (YKC)?

**Audience:** Public users  
**Category:** rewards  
**Related topics:** milestones, ranks, Live Arena, creator economy

## Overview

Yenkasa Coin, also called YKC, is the in-app reward coin used to recognize meaningful participation on Yenkasa.

YKC is part of the platform economy. It is earned through approved actions that help the ecosystem, such as engagement, consistency, and contribution.

## What YKC Represents

YKC is meant to reward:

- activity
- consistency
- support for creators and communities
- positive participation
- long-term contribution

It is not simply a number for passive presence. The system is tied to actual tracked events inside the platform.

## Common Ways Users Earn YKC

Depending on platform rules and limits, users can earn YKC through activities such as:

- creating posts
- receiving approved post rewards
- commenting and replying
- liking content
- following users
- receiving follows
- daily login activity
- watch activity
- community actions
- livestream and Live Arena activity

## Important Guardrails

YKC has anti-abuse controls. That means:

- not every repeated action earns endlessly
- some rewards are rate-limited
- daily caps can apply
- only trusted backend events count

## FAQ

### Is YKC a reward system or just a badge?

It is a reward system tied to tracked platform activity.

### Can inactive users earn the same as active contributors?

No. YKC is designed to reflect participation and contribution.

### Does every action earn the same reward?

No. Different actions can carry different reward values or limits.

## Quick Summary

YKC is Yenkasa’s platform reward coin. It exists to encourage useful engagement, community participation, and creator growth.
