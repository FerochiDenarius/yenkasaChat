# YKC Reward Philosophy

**Audience:** Public users  
**Category:** rewards  
**Related topics:** YKC, milestones, engagement philosophy, creator economy

## Overview

YKC is designed to reward contribution, not noise.

The idea behind the Yenkasa Coin system is simple: people who help the platform become more active, more useful, and more engaging should be recognized.

## What the System Tries to Encourage

YKC is meant to encourage:

- steady participation
- helpful engagement
- creator support
- community activity
- repeat contribution over time

## What the System Tries to Discourage

YKC is not meant to reward:

- spam
- fake repetition
- exploit behavior
- low-trust shortcuts

That is why the system uses backend validation, daily caps, and action-specific limits.

## Why This Matters

Without limits and validation, reward systems become easy to abuse. Yenkasa treats rewards as part of platform trust, not just as a fun counter.

## FAQ

### Why not reward everything equally?

Because not all activity improves the platform in the same way.

### Why are some rewards capped?

Caps reduce abuse and keep the economy more balanced.

### Does YKC reward only creators?

No. It can reward broader participation across the platform.
